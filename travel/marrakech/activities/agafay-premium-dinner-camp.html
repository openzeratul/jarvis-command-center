Why recommended

  • Shared camps can feel packed; premium (Inara / Scarabeo class) sell quieter dinner, better plating, stronger sunset setting.
  • For 4 adults celebrating NYE week, one premium dinner may be the trip’s photo highlight.
  • Skip if budget tight — Best Value GYG ~CAD 41/pp already covers camel+dinner.

Self-drive alternative

  • Day visit / lunch possible by car with GPS to camp.
  • Night dinner: OK for confident drivers; offline maps; signal drops. Fuel ~CAD 9–18 RT est.
  • Many guests still prefer camp-arranged transfer after dark.
