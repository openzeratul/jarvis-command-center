Self-drive DIY

  • Drive Palmeraie → park Guéliz or Bab Doukkala / Koutoubia periphery.
  • Walk to Ensemble Artisanal (Ave Mohammed V near Koutoubia) — fixed prices, no haggling.
  • Then enter souks via Souk Semmarine from Jemaa el-Fna north.
  • Bargaining: open ~40–50% of ask; land ~40–60%; pay MAD not EUR.

Indicative souvenir ranges (Serenity guide sanity check, not guarantees): small lanterns ~100–300 MAD · leather bags after haggling ~200–600 MAD · babouches ~50–150 MAD · ras el hanout 100g ~40–70 MAD. Convert ≈ ×0.148 CAD.

Practical notes / scams

  • Unsolicited guides → commission shops. Fake saffron/argan. Count change.
  • Pickpockets in dense lanes — front pocket / money belt.
  • Co-op: Ensemble Artisanal Mon–Sat ~9:30–19:00, Sun shorter — verify locally.
