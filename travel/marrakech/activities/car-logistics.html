Medina parking (critical)

  • Do not drive into Medina alleys — scratches, dead-ends, stress.
  • Park at supervised lots near Bab Doukkala, Koutoubia, other gates; walk or short taxi.
  • Agree gardien price before leaving car; tip on return.

Example published rates (Guéliz underground): first hour 5 MAD, then 3 MAD/h; 17 MAD/4 h; 40 MAD/24 h (local press 2025–26). Street/gardien day tips often ~3–20 MAD — confirm on site.

Tour pickup vs self-drive

  • Shared tours often price for central Marrakech; Palmeraie may add 50–250 MAD (seen on Agafay GYG notes).
  • With rental already paid, DIY Ourika and daytime Agafay maximize value.
  • Keep organized for: first Medina orientation, sunset Agafay dinner, NYE night.

Practical / safety

  • Don’t hand keys to random “helpers” without agreeing fee. Photograph car on parking handover.
  • Speed bumps and scooters everywhere — defensive driving.
  • Rental contract not researched here (user already committed YES). Confirm CDW, second driver, any Atlas restrictions.
