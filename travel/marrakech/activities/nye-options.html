Why this matters

  • First night of the trip is NYE — jet lag likely.
  • Frugal friends may prefer resort AI over 1,500–3,800+ MAD/pp galas.
  • Decide early: structured city booking vs low-key resort.

Option A — Resort (Value / Practical default)

  • Stay put: pools, AI dinner, on-site entertainment if offered.
  • Unverified: whether Be Live runs special NYE gala / dress code / surcharge — ask hotel before travel.
  • Pros: zero taxi, no Medina chaos, inclusive drinks (per AI rules). Cons: less “Marrakech” atmosphere.

Action: email reservas.marrakech@belivehotels.com or ask at check-in about 31 Dec program.

Option B — Jemaa el-Fna countdown (Practical soft)

  • Spontaneous crowd; no single official municipal concert (2026/27 guides).
  • Pros: free, memorable. Cons: extreme crowds, pickpockets, hard exit after midnight, tired after travel day.
  • Hotel shuttle or parked car + walk; pre-arrange late return to Palmeraie. Short look only.

Option D — Agafay NYE special

Some desert camps sell NYE dinners/parties. Expect Premium pricing and sell-outs. Only if group explicitly wants desert NYE; otherwise do Agafay on 2–4 Jan.

Organized vs self-drive

  • NYE night: avoid DIY desert drives after midnight.
  • City: taxi/shuttle better than hunting parking at 01:00.

Cancellation / recommendation

  1. Default: quiet resort NYE (jet lag + value).
  2. If energy: early dinner at resort → short Jemaa look → early exit.
  3. Splurge path: one prepaid rooftop or save for Agafay Premium later — don’t do both unless budget allows.

Gala deposits often strict — read terms. Resort: usually included unless special ticketed event.
