Self-drive DIY

  • Drive Palmeraie → park at Bab Doukkala, Koutoubia-area, or Guéliz; walk or petit taxi into Medina.
  • Do not attempt Medina alley driving.
  • DIY without guide is free but higher risk of commission touts.
  • Time: 20–35 min drive + parking; walking circuit 2–4 h.
  • Cost estimate for 4: parking ~20–80 MAD half-day (unverified exact lot) + optional taxi.

Cancellation

GYG listings: full refund if cancel ≥24 hours before start. Viator private: typically same 24 h — confirm on product page.

Practical notes

  • Dress: shoulders/knees covered; comfortable shoes; light layers (Dec–Jan evenings cool).
  • Cash MAD for tips/water. Decline unsolicited guides; “square is closed” detours.
  • Day ~16–21°C Dec–Jan; jacket after dark.
  • Use hotel shuttle or drive+park — walking from Palmeraie not realistic.
