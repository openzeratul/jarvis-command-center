Gardens — Majorelle & Le Jardin Secret

Best Value Kestrel Value Kestrel Practical (Menara)

Low-effort half day; photogenic; good recovery after Ourika/Agafay; no spa needed.

From briefSlug jardins-majorelle-and-secret
Price caveat: Majorelle prices checked via guide citing official Jun 2026 — re-check official ticket site before travel. MAD ≈ 0.148 CAD (approx).

Why recommended

  • Cheap relative to tours; self-drive or taxi friendly from Palmeraie.
  • Majorelle is bucket-list; Secret Garden is calmer Medina green space.
  • Menara add-on is free grounds for sunset photos (Practical).

1) Jardin Majorelle

Rue Yves Saint Laurent, Guéliz · Daily 8:00–18:30 (last entry 18:00). Online tickets only — no gate office.

Under 10 free. Time: 45–60 min garden; half day if + museums. Book first slot — expect crowds. Drive Palmeraie → Majorelle ~15–25 min; Guéliz street parking or petit taxi.

2) Le Jardin Secret

Rue Mouassine 121, Medina · Nov–Jan hours 9:30–18:00. Admission 100 MAD adult; 80 MAD ≤24; under 12 free. Tower +40 MAD.

For 4 garden only: 400 MAD ≈ CAD 59 / EUR 37.

Tickets non-refundable; valid 1 year from purchase (FAQ). Park outside Medina; combine with souks.

3) Menara Gardens (bonus)

Olive groves + reflecting pool; pavilion may charge (~60 MAD foreign adult per 2026 guides — verify). Grounds often free stroll. Drive ~20–30 min from Palmeraie.

Organized vs self-drive

  • Majorelle often bundled into city tours (entries usually extra). Best as self-guided timed entry.
  • Self-drive Guéliz for Majorelle; Medina edge for Secret.

Cancellation / reviews

Majorelle: follow timed-entry terms on official site. Jardin Secret: non-refundable per FAQ. Sources: official Fondation + planetwhitley practical guide updated Sep 2026.

Practical notes

  • Save Majorelle QR offline; no large luggage.
  • Winter mornings crisp — light jacket.
  • Combine: Majorelle morning → lunch → Medina Secret + light shopping.
