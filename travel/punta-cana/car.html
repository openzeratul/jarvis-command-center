Safety notes

  • Canada advice (updated 2026-09-24): consider licensed driver; accidents frequent; holiday traffic worse at Easter.
  • Avoid night driving outside lit tourist corridors; Semana Santa = highest self-drive risk window.
  • Canadian licence OK short tourist use; IDP recommended by travel.gc.ca.
  • Never leave valuables in cars; Cap Cana gates may need guest registration.
